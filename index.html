HabitGrid Guide
Everything you need to know
🚀 Getting Started
1
Set your name — Go to Settings → Edit to personalise your greeting.
2
Add your first habit — Tap the ✏️ Edit button on the dashboard, then "Add Habit". Give it a name, icon & colour.
3
Check in daily — Tap a habit card on the Today screen to mark it done. A confetti burst means you nailed it!
4
Track your progress — Visit the 📅 History and 📊 Stats tabs to see streaks, trends, and monthly heat maps.
📱 Navigating the App
🏠
Today — Your daily habit checklist. Tap to check off, tap ✏️ Edit to add or remove habits.
📅
History — Monthly calendar view. Tap any day to see exactly what was completed.
📊
Stats — Completion rates, current streaks, best streaks, and 8-week trends per habit.
⚙️
Settings — Profile, manage habits, export data, and this Help guide.
💡 Pro Tips
🔥 Streaks reset if you miss a day — check in every evening to protect your chain.
📆 Back-filling — Tap any past day in History to toggle habits for that date (up to 30 days back).
📤 Export your data as Excel or PDF anytime from Settings before clearing your browser storage.
🎯 Start small — Research shows 3–5 habits is the sweet spot for long-term success. Quality over quantity!
📜 Rules & Guidelines
HabitGrid stores all data locally on your device — your data never leaves your browser.
You may have up to 20 active habits at a time for best performance and clarity.
Completing a habit is honour-based — only mark it done when you genuinely did it.
Resetting data is permanent and cannot be undone. Always export a backup first.
Habit names must be 42 characters or fewer. Keep them short and actionable.
HabitGrid is a personal productivity tool. Please use it responsibly and respect your own pace.
